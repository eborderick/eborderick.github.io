Equine Analytics Hub

Data-Driven Decision Making for Horse Owners

Hi, I'm Eloise. As a horse owner, I know firsthand how overwhelming it can be to navigate the endless, often conflicting advice and opinions that circulate on livery yards. When making critical choices for our horses, it can be incredibly difficult to cut through the noise.

This hub is my passion project and a portfolio of my work as a developer. My goal is to replace yard gossip with objective, evidence-based data. By combining modern web development with peer-reviewed equine research, I build lightweight software tools that give owners clear, actionable metrics to make confident, welfare-first decisions.
